2. Prepayment, Property Sale, and Early Redemption Terms
SELLING THE PROPERTY OR REDEEMING THE LOAN WITHIN LOCK-IN:
If you sell the mortgaged property or refinance/prepay this housing loan within the 36-month Commitment Period (including within 6 months of disbursement), substantial early redemption charges and subsidy clawbacks apply as set out below.
Clause 7.1: Prepayment Penalty Fee on Early Redemption

If the Borrower prepays or redeems the Facility in full or in part within the thirty-six (36) months Commitment (Lock-In) Period commencing from the date of the first disbursement of the Loan (whether such redemption occurs as a result of refinancing, personal prepayment, or upon the sale or disposal of the Property):
A prepayment penalty fee of 1.50% of the outstanding loan amount prepaid or redeemed shall be immediately levied and payable to the Bank upon redemption.

Clause 7.2: Clawback of Bank Cash Subsidies

In the event that the Facility is redeemed in full within thirty-six (36) months from the date of first disbursement for any reason whatsoever (including upon sale of the Property):
The Borrower shall immediately reimburse and pay back to the Bank in full all legal fee subsidies (SGD 2,500.00) and valuation subsidies (SGD 500.00) disbursed by the Bank, amounting to a total clawback of SGD 3,000.00.

Clause 7.3: Prior Written Notice Requirement

The Borrower must give the Bank at least three (3) months' prior written notice (or pay three (3) months' interest in lieu of such notice) before making any full or partial prepayment of the loan facility.

Clause 7.4: Worked Example — Sale within 6 Months

If the Borrower sells the property within 6 months of loan drawdown with an outstanding balance of SGD 535,000.00, the total penalties payable upon redemption consist of:
(a) 1.50% Prepayment Penalty on SGD 535,000.00 = SGD 8,025.00
(b) Legal & Valuation Subsidy Clawback = SGD 3,000.00
(c) Redemption Administrative Handling Fee = SGD 500.00
Total Penalty upon sale within 6 months: SGD 11,525.00 (plus 3 months notice or interest in lieu).
