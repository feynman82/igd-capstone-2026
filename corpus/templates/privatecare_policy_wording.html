4. Cardiovascular & Stent Procedures (Clinical Specifics)
Clause 4.1: Coronary Angioplasty and Heart Stent Insertion

Where the Insured undergoes medically indicated Percutaneous Transluminal Coronary Angioplasty (PTCA) with primary or elective stent insertion (including Bare-Metal Stents and Drug-Eluting Stents) for coronary artery disease, the procedure, specialist surgical fees, operating theatre costs, catheterisation lab charges, and all approved stent devices are COVERED AS CHARGED under Table of Surgical Operations (Class 5), subject to the Annual Deductible of SGD {{excess}} and 10% co-insurance.

Clause 4.2: Kidney Stones & Urological Interventions

Inpatient surgical management of kidney stones (nephrolithiasis), including Percutaneous Nephrolithotomy (PCNL), Ureteroscopy (URS) with laser stone fragmentation, and open/laparoscopic stone extraction requiring overnight hospitalisation, is covered as charged. Outpatient Extracorporeal Shock Wave Lithotripsy (ESWL) is covered up to a maximum of five (5) sessions per policy year under Day Surgery provisions.

6. General Exclusions (What is Not Covered)

THE FOLLOWING CONDITIONS, PROCEDURES, AND TREATMENTS ARE STRICTLY EXCLUDED FROM COVERAGE UNDER THIS POLICY. NO BENEFIT SHALL BE PAYABLE IN RESPECT OF:

  1. Pre-existing Conditions: Any illness, disease, impairment, or physical defect that existed or showed symptoms prior to the inception date of this Policy, unless declared in the application and explicitly accepted in writing by the Insurer without exclusions.
  2. Experimental or Unproven Treatments: Medical treatments, surgical procedures, medicines, or devices that are experimental, investigative, or not approved by the Xinkapow Health Sciences Authority (HSA).
  3. Cosmetic, Aesthetic, or Plastic Surgery: Any elective procedure for aesthetic enhancement, reshaping, breast augmentation/reduction, or hair restoration, unless reconstructive surgery necessitated by accidental injury occurring while insured.
  4. Routine Examinations & Preventive Health: Routine physical health screenings, annual medical check-ups, general health screenings, vaccinations, and non-symptomatic diagnostic investigations.
  5. Dental Care & Appliances: Dental check-ups, extraction, crowns, dentures, braces, implants, and periodontic treatment, except emergency surgical treatment to sound natural teeth damaged solely by external traumatic accident.
  6. Self-Inflicted Injury & Substance Abuse: Deliberate self-injury, suicide or attempted suicide, or illnesses/injuries resulting directly or indirectly from alcoholism, narcotics, or substance abuse.
  7. Pregnancy, Childbirth & Fertility: Antenatal care, normal delivery, Caesarean section, miscarriage, abortion, contraception, sterilisation, fertility treatments, or assisted reproduction technologies (IVF).
  8. Congenital Anomalies: Hereditary or congenital conditions diagnosed within twenty-four (24) months from the birth of the Insured, unless covered under an approved congenital rider.
  9. Psychiatric & Mental Disorders: Treatment for psychiatric conditions, depression, anxiety, insomnia, behavioural disorders, or psychogeriatric diseases.
  10. Alternative & Complementary Medicine: Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM), acupuncture, herbal remedies, chiropractic treatment, osteopathy, naturopathy, and Ayurvedic treatment.
  11. Refractive Errors: Lasik, refractive keratotomy, eye examinations, spectacles, or contact lenses for correction of myopia, hyperopia, or astigmatism.
  12. Sexually Transmitted Diseases: Testing, treatment, or management of venereal diseases, HIV, AIDS, or related complex infections.
  13. War, Terrorism & Military Service: Injuries or illness resulting from active involvement in war, invasion, armed rebellion, military training, or nuclear contamination.
  14. Rest Cures & Convalescence: Hospital confinement primarily for nursing care, rest cure, sanatorium care, rehabilitation without active medical treatment, or hospice care.
  15. Non-Prescription / Dietary Supplements: Vitamins, weight-loss products, nutritional tonics, and over-the-counter health supplements.
