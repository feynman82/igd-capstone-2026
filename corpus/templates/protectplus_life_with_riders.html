3. Basic Benefit
3.1 Death Benefit

In the event of the death of the Life Assured while this policy remains in full force and effect during the Period of Insurance, the Company shall pay the Basic Sum Assured stated in the Policy Schedule to the designated beneficiaries, legal executors, or administrators. Payment of the death benefit is subject to the submission of satisfactory proof of death and admission of the claim by the Company. Upon settlement of the death benefit under this section, the policy and all underlying coverages shall immediately terminate.

4. Rider Benefits
4.1 Critical Illness Rider

The sum assured under the Critical Illness Rider is payable in a single lump sum upon the definitive medical diagnosis of the Life Assured with any of the defined major critical illnesses covered under the contract specifications, provided the diagnosis is certified by an accredited specialist physician and accepted by the Company during the Period of Insurance.

4.2 Early Critical Illness Rider

The Early Critical Illness Rider pays on early-stage diagnosis of a covered condition as certified by an appropriate registered medical specialist. Any payment made under the Early Critical Illness Rider reduces the Critical Illness Rider by the amount paid, with all future coverage under the Critical Illness Rider operating strictly on the reduced balance.

4.3 Total Permanent Disability Rider

The sum assured under the Total Permanent Disability Rider is payable in the event that the Life Assured suffers total and irrecoverable disability resulting from bodily injury or illness that entirely prevents the Life Assured from engaging in any employment, occupation, or profession for wages, compensation, or profit for the remainder of their lifetime.

4.4 Hospital Cash Rider

The Hospital Cash Rider pays the specified daily benefit per day of inpatient stay when the Life Assured is confined to a licensed hospital on the recommendation of a registered medical practitioner for medically necessary treatment of an injury or illness. The benefit is payable up to a maximum of 365 days per admission, after which daily benefits for that specific confinement cease.

5. Premium Payment and Grace Period
5.1 Payment of Premium

Premiums are payable annually in advance on or before the due dates specified in the Policy Schedule. The receipt of premium by the Company is an absolute condition precedent to the validity of any cover or continued liability under this contract.

5.2 Grace Period

A grace period of 30 days is allowed for the payment of each renewal premium following its due date[cite: 16]. During this 30-day grace period, the policy and all attached rider benefits remain in full force[cite: 16]. If a valid claim arises during the grace period, any overdue premium then payable will be deducted from the claim proceeds. If any premium remains unpaid at the expiration of the 30 days, the policy shall immediately lapse and cease to provide coverage[cite: 16].

6. Exclusions

No benefits under the policy or any attached supplementary riders shall be payable under the following circumstances:

6.1 Suicide

If the Life Assured commits suicide, whether sane or insane, within 12 months from the inception date or reinstatement date of the policy, the Company's liability shall be limited strictly to a refund of all premiums paid without interest, less any indebtedness.

6.2 Pre-Existing Conditions

Any injury, illness, condition, or symptom for which medical advice, diagnosis, care, or treatment was received, recommended, or foreseeable prior to the commencement of the policy is expressly excluded, unless fully declared in the application and formally accepted in writing by the Company[cite: 7, 23].

6.3 War and Civil Commotion

Any death, disability, illness, or hospitalisation directly or indirectly resulting from war, declared or undeclared, invasion, act of foreign enemy, hostilities, civil war, rebellion, revolution, or military usurped power.

6.4 Self-Inflicted Injury

Any bodily injury, impairment, or illness intentionally self-inflicted by the Life Assured, whether sane or insane, or arising from any willful exposure to exceptional peril[cite: 23].

7. Free-Look Period

If the Policyholder is not completely satisfied with this policy, the Policyholder may return the policy contract to the Company for cancellation within a free-look period of 14 days from the date of receipt of this document[cite: 9]. Upon such cancellation within the 14 days, the Company will refund all premiums paid, less any medical examination expenses and administrative charges incurred by the Company in issuing the policy[cite: 9].
