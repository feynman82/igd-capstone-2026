Interface design pack · Project PARROT

Designing an assistant that speaks first

User stories from the five personas, the information architecture they imply, hi-fidelity screens, component states and a motion specification. Built to be handed to a coding agent without a second conversation.

01

The design read

This is not a landing page and it is not a chat app. It is a private record system with a conversational surface, used in short bursts by people who are already overloaded. That reading produces one decision everything else follows from.

The home screen is not a text box

Every AI product opens on an empty prompt. For these five personas that is precisely wrong: they are time-poor, they do not know what to ask, and the Reminder and Alert modules are proactive. Sarah does not want to ask whether there is a school event this week. She wants to be told. So PARROT opens on Today, a surface that shows what needs attention, and chat lives one tab away for the times she has a specific question. Retrieval is still the core of the product; Today is what the Reminder and Alert modules put on top of it.

Principle 01

Answer, then prove it

No factual claim appears without a source chip linking to the document and page it came from. Trust is the product. The citation is where it lives.

Principle 02

Silence beats a guess

When nothing in the vault supports an answer, the interface says so in a visually distinct state. Abstention is a designed screen, not an error.

Principle 03

Correction is a first-class flow

Extraction will be wrong sometimes. Fixing a field takes one click from the place the mistake is visible, and the fix is remembered.

Principle 04

Three clicks, from your team's brief

Any stored fact is reachable in three interactions from Today. This is an acceptance criterion, not an aspiration: it gets tested.

Principle 05

Capture is where the user already is

Nobody opens an app to file a receipt. They photograph it in a chat app or drop it in a folder. The app is for reading, not filing.

Principle 06

Nothing moves without a reason

This is a daily tool, not a demo. Motion is reserved for state changes and entry. Everything the user does often is instant.

02

User stories

Written from your team's five personas, one per persona, each tied to a capability module or the RAG core, and to a screen. Acceptance criteria are testable, which matters because they are what a coding agent will build against.

Sarah, 35 · Reminder module · Today

As a parent juggling two school calendars, I want to be told about renewals and school dates before they arrive, so that I stop discovering them the morning they happen.

Accepts when: an obligation with a due date inside its lead window appears at the top of Today, ordered by urgency, with the source document one click away, and can be dismissed or snoozed without leaving the screen.

Marcus, 39 · RAG core · Ask

As the person managing my father's care, I want to ask which insurer covered his last admission and get an answer I can verify, so that I can make a claim without opening six folders.

Accepts when: the answer names the insurer and policy number, each carries a source chip that opens the document at the correct page, and the system abstains rather than guessing when no document covers the question.

Daniel and Rachel · Capture · Reminder module

As a couple with no time to file anything, we want to photograph a document in a chat app and have it filed and understood, so that filing costs us fifteen seconds instead of an evening.

Accepts when: a photo sent to the PARROT bot returns a confirmation naming the document type and the key date it extracted, within 60 seconds, and the document appears in the vault with those fields populated.

Priya, 32 · Alert module · Today

As someone contributing to SRS, I want to be warned before I miss a top-up window or approach the relief cap, so that I stop losing money to a deadline I forgot.

Accepts when: crossing 90% of a threshold raises a single alert on Today, states the current figure and the ceiling, cites the documents the figure was computed from, and does not repeat daily.

Jason, 40 · Tracker module · Today

As a business owner whose personal and business spending blur together, I want a monthly view of household spend by category, so that I walk into tax season with the numbers already separated.

Accepts when: the figure is computed in SQL from typed rows rather than generated text, the underlying receipts are one click away, and any uncategorised item is visible rather than silently excluded.

03

Information architecture

Four surfaces. The count is deliberate: a fifth would require a "more" menu, and a household tool that needs a "more" menu has already lost the people it was built for.

04

Today

The flagship screen and the one to demo first. It answers the question the personas actually have, which is not "what can I ask you" but "what have I forgotten".

05

Ask, and the two answers it can give

The conversational surface exists for Marcus's question, the one that has a specific answer buried in a specific document. Two states matter more than anything else here: the grounded answer with its sources, and the refusal.

Detail that matters

Citation chips are the trust surface

They name the document and the page, not an index number. Tapping one opens the document at that page with the supporting passage highlighted. If a claim has no chip, it does not ship.

Detail that matters

Suggestions come from this week's ingest

An empty chat with three generic prompts is filler. Three questions generated from documents that arrived in the last seven days are genuinely useful, and they demonstrate that ingestion is live.

06

Vault, and the correction loop

Extraction will be wrong. Designing for that honestly is what separates a system people keep using from one they abandon after the third bad date. Low-confidence fields are visually flagged before the user is surprised by them.

09

Motion specification

A daily household tool should feel instant, not animated. The rule applied throughout: the more often a user sees an interaction, the less it moves.

ElementMovementDurationEasingReason
Any button pressscale(0.97)160msease-outConfirms the interface heard the press. The one animation that is always worth it.
Today rows on loadopacity 0 to 1, translateY(8px)300ms, 50ms staggercubic-bezier(.23,1,.32,1)Seen once per session. The stagger reads as ordering by urgency.
Source chip revealopacity, scale(0.96) to 1180msease-outMarks the moment the answer became verifiable. Fires once, after streaming ends.
Document preview openscale(0.96) to 1 from the chip220msease-outtransform-origin set to the chip, so the panel grows out of what was clicked.
Row dismiss or snoozetranslateX + height collapse200msease-outCSS transition, never keyframes, so rapid dismissals retarget instead of restarting.
Tab switch, Today to Asknone0msn/aUsed dozens of times a day. Animation here makes the app feel slow.
Any keyboard-triggered actionnone0msn/aNever animate a keyboard action. It is the fastest way to make a fast app feel sluggish.
Skeleton shimmerbackground-position1400ms looplinearConstant motion takes linear. Collapses to a static tint under reduced motion.

Three rules behind the table. Animate only transform and opacity, so everything runs off the main thread while the ingestion worker is busy. Never enter from scale(0); start at 0.96 with opacity, because nothing in the physical world appears from nothing. And gate every hover effect behind @media (hover: hover) and (pointer: fine), since a touch device fires hover on tap and produces effects that stick.

Under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce, keep the opacity transitions that aid comprehension and remove every positional one. Reduced motion means gentler, not absent.

10

Build order

Five screens, but not five weeks. The order matters because each one unblocks a demo you can show a supervisor.

OrderScreenUnblocksEffort
1Ask, with citations and the abstention stateProves retrieval works and the trust story is real. Demoable the day retrieval lands.2 days
2Vault document detail with editable fieldsProves extraction works, and gives you the correction loop that feeds evaluation labels.2 days
3TodayThe differentiating screen. Needs obligations and the scheduler, so it comes after extraction.3 days
4PacksMakes the platform argument visible. Mostly a list view over the pack registry.1 day
5States and polishEmpty, loading, error, quarantine. The day that makes it feel finished.1 day

The order also matches the agreed build scope. Ask lands while the RAG core is the only thing running, in week 4. Vault arrives once typed extraction is in, in week 4 to 5. Today needs the Reminder and Alert modules, so it follows them in week 7. Packs is the last functional screen because it has nothing to list until Tax Assistant and Insurance Agent exist.

Stack note for Antigravity

Next.js 15 with the App Router, Tailwind and shadcn/ui. shadcn shipped dedicated chat primitives in June 2026 (MessageScroller, Message, Bubble, Attachment, Marker) that work directly with useChat from the AI SDK, which removes most of the work in screen 1. Do not ship them in their default state: set the radius scale, the palette and the type once in the theme, then let the agent generate against it. One accent colour, one radius system, one type scale, locked before the first screen.
