# Incident log

> Owner: Siew. The procedure is `incident-and-breach-procedure.md`, and this log is its section 4.
> Approved 2026-10-05.
>
> **Every** incident is logged here, including minor ones and ones assessed as not notifiable.
> The log is the evidence for the Accountability Obligation.
> **Never write personal data in this file.** Name a document by its id and a person by their
> role ("the owner", "a household member", "a third party").

## Template

```
### INC-<yyyy-mm-dd>-<n>: <one-line title>
- Noticed:     <date and time>, by <role>, through <how>
- Nature:      <kind, from procedure section 1>; <what happened>
- Data:        <categories, by inventory code, e.g. D2, D5>; <number of people affected>
- Contained:   <measures taken, and when>; incident hold placed on <records> (retention A6)
- Assessment:  <how it was assessed: what was checked, by whom, when>
               breach yes/no; notifiable yes/no (significant harm? significant scale?); why
- Notified:    <PDPC within 3 calendar days / individuals / supervisors, and when>,
               or "not required, because ..."
- Cause:       <root cause>
- Remediation: <long-term actions to prevent a recurrence: commit, test, Set C item, ADR>
- Closed:      <date>; incident hold released
```
