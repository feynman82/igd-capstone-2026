## Definitions

1. **“System”** means PARROT.
2. **“System Developer”** means the PARROT team, being the party that designs, builds, tests and
   maintains the System. *(The team's members are listed in `team/README.md`.)*
3. **“System Deployer”** means the PARROT team, being the party that releases the System to
   users and sets up its environment.
4. **“User”** means any individual who submits any document, information or instruction
   through the System, including a Submitting Party.

## Clause A. Authority and Consent for Submissions on Behalf of Another Adult

1. **Authority to Act.** Where an adult (“Submitting Party”) completes, signs, submits, or
   otherwise provides any document, form, declaration, information or instruction through the
   System on behalf of another adult (“Represented Party”), the Submitting Party represents and
   warrants that they have obtained all necessary authority, consent and authorisation from the
   Represented Party to do so.

2. **Deemed Authorisation.** To the fullest extent permitted by applicable law, any document,
   information, declaration, consent or instruction submitted by the Submitting Party on behalf
   of the Represented Party shall be deemed to have been submitted with the Represented Party’s
   authority and consent. The System Developer may rely on such submission without
   independently verifying the identity, authority or consent of the Submitting Party or
   Represented Party, unless otherwise required by law.

3. **Responsibility for Accuracy.** The Submitting Party shall be responsible for ensuring that
   all information, declarations, consents and documents submitted on behalf of the Represented
   Party are complete, accurate and duly authorised. The System Developer shall not be
   responsible for verifying the accuracy, completeness or validity of information or documents
   supplied by the Submitting Party.

4. **Disputes Between Parties.** Any dispute between the Submitting Party and the Represented
   Party concerning the authority to act, the existence or scope of consent, the accuracy of
   information submitted, or the validity of any document or transaction shall be a matter
   between those parties. To the fullest extent permitted by applicable law, the System
   Developer shall have no liability arising from or in connection with such dispute.

5. **Indemnity.** The Submitting Party shall indemnify and hold harmless the System Developer,
   its officers, employees, agents and contractors from and against any claims, losses,
   liabilities, damages, costs and expenses (including reasonable legal costs) arising out of
   or in connection with:
   - (a) any unauthorised or purportedly authorised submission made on behalf of the
     Represented Party;
   - (b) any dispute between the Submitting Party and Represented Party concerning such
     submission;
   - (c) any inaccurate, incomplete or misleading information provided by the Submitting
     Party; or
   - (d) any breach by the Submitting Party of this clause.

6. **No Independent Verification.** The Submitting Party acknowledges that the System
   Developer is entitled to rely on the information, representations and authorisations
   provided through the System and is not required to independently investigate or verify the
   relationship, authority or consent between the parties, except where such verification is
   expressly required under applicable law or agreed contractual requirements.

7. **Limitation of Liability.** To the fullest extent permitted by applicable law, the System
   Developer shall not be liable for any direct, indirect, incidental, consequential or special
   loss, damage, cost or expense arising from or relating to any dispute, representation,
   submission or transaction between the Submitting Party and the Represented Party, including
   any dispute concerning whether the Represented Party authorised or consented to the
   submission.

8. **Mandatory Rights Preserved.** Nothing in this clause shall exclude or limit any liability,
   obligation or right that cannot lawfully be excluded or limited under applicable law.

## Clause B. Purpose of Collection, Use and Retention

> *Drafted at Siew's request on 2026-10-01 (decision 5d: one purpose statement that names all
> four uses), restyled to match Clause A, and **approved by Siew on 2026-10-02**, together with
> the definition of "User".*

1. **Purpose.** Any document, information or personal data submitted by a User through the
   System shall be collected, used and retained solely for the purpose of assisting the User’s
   household to understand and keep track of its own documents (the “Purpose”).

2. **Permitted Uses.** In furtherance of the Purpose, the System may:
   - (a) answer questions asked by the User concerning the User’s documents, citing the page
     of the document from which each answer is drawn;
   - (b) remind the User, without request, of dates contained in the User’s documents,
     including renewal dates and payment due dates;
   - (c) alert the User, without request, where a figure contained in the User’s documents
     approaches an applicable limit, including tax relief utilised against its cap; or
   - (d) calculate and explain advice, including any shortfall in insurance cover, provided
     that such advice shall be given only in respect of an individual who has separately
     given, and has not withdrawn, consent to receive advice.

3. **No Action on Behalf of the User.** The System shall not make any payment, booking,
   renewal, filing or other transaction on behalf of the User. The System provides information
   and advice only.

4. **No Other Use.** The System shall not collect, use or retain any document, information or
   personal data for any purpose other than the Purpose and the uses set out in paragraph 2.

## Clause C. Personal Data of Third Parties

> *Drafted at Siew's request on 2026-10-02 from decisions already recorded in
> `data-inventory.md` §6, 5c: the definition of a third party, consent or contractual agreement
> as the basis, and the business contact exclusion. **Approved by Siew on 2026-10-02.***

1. **Third Party.** In this clause, “Third Party” means an individual whose personal data
   appears on any document submitted through the System and who is neither the Submitting
   Party nor a member of the User’s household as recorded in the System. An individual whose
   relationship to the household has not been confirmed in the System shall be treated as a
   Third Party until it is confirmed.

2. **Consent or Contractual Entitlement.** Where any document submitted through the System
   contains personal data of a Third Party, the User represents and warrants that the User has
   obtained the Third Party’s consent to the collection, use and retention of such personal
   data by the System for the Purpose, or is otherwise entitled to provide such personal data
   for the Purpose under a contractual agreement with the Third Party.

3. **Business Contact Information.** Paragraph 2 shall not apply to the business contact
   information of a Third Party, being the Third Party’s name, business title, business
   telephone number, business address, business email, business fax or any other similar
   information which is not provided by the Third Party solely for personal purposes,
   including the licence number of an insurance agent and the name and clinic details of a
   medical practitioner.

4. **Mandatory Rights Preserved.** Nothing in this clause shall exclude or limit any
   liability, obligation or right that cannot lawfully be excluded or limited under applicable
   law.

## Clause D. AI-Generated Answers and Helpdesk

> *Drafted at Siew's request on 2026-10-05, and **approved by Siew on 2026-10-05**.*

1. **AI-Generated Answers.** The answers and explanations provided by the System, and the
   information the System reads from submitted documents, are generated by artificial
   intelligence. They may be inaccurate, incomplete or out of date.

2. **Verification by the User.** The User shall verify the accuracy of any answer, explanation
   or advice against the source document cited with it before relying on it. The System
   Developer shall not be responsible for any reliance placed on an answer that the User has
   not verified.

3. **Helpdesk.** If the System is not acting properly, the User may contact the PARROT
   helpdesk at **+65 9123 4567**.

4. **Mandatory Rights Preserved.** Nothing in this clause shall exclude or limit any
   liability, obligation or right that cannot lawfully be excluded or limited under applicable
   law.
