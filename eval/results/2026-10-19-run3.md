**What to read into it.**
- **The casual-phrasing gap has closed:** 8/10 casual against 64/83 formal. It was 4/10 against
  30/40 on 14 October.
- **Retrieval misses:** two, `a-b21` and `a-b23`. Every other Set A miss had its page among the
  passages read.
- **Twelve Set B questions are still answered** (`claimed`): the same family as before (b-b04,
  b-b06, b-b12, b-b14 and others). The label check of ADR 0021 is built but switched off (#99).
- **Extraction is 0.949; 0.982 without one register convention.** On the renewal notices, claim
  letters, benefit illustrations and anniversary statements, the extractor copies the insurer as
  printed ("Meridian Assurance Singapore Pte. Ltd."), while the register records the short name.
  `core/documents.yaml` defines the field as printed, so the register departs from it. This
  accounts for 11 wrong and 11 missed `insurer` fields. Raised on #108, not resolved before the
  freeze.
- **The alert rate is a person-name finding, not an alert bug.** Both alerts that fired are false
  for one reason: the new renewal notices print the insured as "Mr Marcus Lim Jun Wei". The title
  is copied into the subject, which then matches nobody in the household. So his home and car
  policies were reported uncovered for 262 and 233 days. It is the only titled subject in the
  vault. The one obligation scored "the wrong person" (precision 0.875) has the same cause.
  Stripping titles from person fields at extraction, or when matching names, would fix both.
- **Faithfulness and context precision are lower than run 2's.** The vault is 3.5 times larger,
  and Set A is 93 questions rather than 39. That is a harder test, not a regression; the two
  runs share no corpus to compare on.
