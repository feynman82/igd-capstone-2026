SELLING OR TRADING IN THE CAR WITHIN 6 MONTHS:
The vehicle remains the legal property of Crescent Bank Limited until the hire purchase facility is fully discharged. Early settlement within 6 months triggers a 20% penalty deduction from the Rule of 78 interest rebate, a flat early termination penalty fee, and administrative discharge fees.
Clause 7.1: Restriction on Sale or Transfer of Vehicle

The Hirer shall not sell, assign, transfer, charge, sub-hire, or part with possession of the Motor Vehicle (SLK 8842 D) without first making full early settlement of all outstanding hire purchase balances and obtaining a formal Certificate of Discharge and release of vehicle financing financing caveat from the Bank.

Clause 7.2: Early Settlement Interest Rebate (Rule of 78) & 20% Bank Penalty

Where the Hirer settles the facility prior to the expiry of the 84-month tenure:
An interest rebate on unearned interest charges is calculated in accordance with the statutory Rule of 78 formula. However, pursuant to Section 14 of the Hire Purchase Regulations, the Bank retains an administrative deduction of 20% of the statutory interest rebate as an early redemption penalty. The Hirer shall only be credited 80% of the calculated interest rebate.

Clause 7.3: Early Termination Penalty Fee within 12 Months

If early settlement of the hire purchase facility occurs within twelve (12) months from the commencement date (including within 6 months):
The Hirer shall pay an Early Termination Penalty Fee of 1.00% of the outstanding financed balance or SGD 500.00, whichever is higher, to compensate the Bank for origination and administrative overheads.

Clause 7.4: Discharge Administration Fee

A flat documentation and financing caveat release fee of SGD 150.00 is payable on all vehicle redemptions.

Clause 7.5: Summary — Penalties if You Sell Within 6 Months

If Marcus Lim sells the car within 6 months of taking out the loan, he must pay:
1. 20% penalty deduction from his unearned interest rebate under Rule of 78.
2. Early Termination Fee: 1.00% of outstanding loan balance (approx. SGD 610.00).
3. Discharge Administrative Fee: SGD 150.00.
All outstanding principal must be paid before title transfers to the new buyer.
