Integrated Shield Plans: Integrated Shield Plans (IPs) comprise two components: (i) A MediShield Life component run by the Central Provident Fund Board (CPFB) and (ii) additional private insurance coverage run by the private insurer. The MediShield Life component provides coverage targeted at B2/C￾wards in public hospitals, while the additional private insurance coverage provides additional coverage beyond MediShield Life coverage. IPs therefore do not provide duplicate coverage with MediShield Life. This table shows the Standard IP benefits, targeted at Class B1 wards in public hospitals. The 
benefits of the Standard IP are identical across all IP insurers.
