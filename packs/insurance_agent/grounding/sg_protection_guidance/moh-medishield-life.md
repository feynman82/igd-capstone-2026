## Premium Subsidy for the Lower- to Middle-Income

For those living in residences with Annual Value of $31,000 or less

Individuals who own more than one property are not eligible for Premium Subsidies.

Permanent Residents receive half of the applicable subsidy rates for Singapore Citizens.

Table B: Premium Subsidies

| Lower Income $0 - $1,500 | Lower Middle Income $1,501 - $2,600 | Upper Middle Income $2,601 - $3,600 |

| 1 - 40 | 25% | 20% | 15% |

| 41 - 50 | 30% | 25% | 20% |

| 51 - 60 | 35% | 30% | 20% |

| 61 - 70 | 40% | 35% | 25% |

| 71 - 80 | 45% | 40% | 30% |

| 81 - 85 | 50% | 45% | 30% |

| 86 - 90 | 55% | 50% | 35% |

| > 90 | 60% | 55% | 40% |

Note: Those living in residences with an Annual Value of between $21,001 and $31,000 will receive 10 percentage points less than the subsidy rates shown above. Those living in the residences with an Annual Value of above $31,000 will not receive these subsidies
