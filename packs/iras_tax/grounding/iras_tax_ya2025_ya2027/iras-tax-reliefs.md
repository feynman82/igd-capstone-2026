Tax reliefs
You are eligible for personal reliefs and rebates if you are a Singapore Tax Resident and if you fulfilled the qualifying conditions of the
reliefs and rebates.
Answer a few simple questions to find out what personal reliefs you are eligible for.
Note: Only a tax resident (including non-Singapore Citizens who are in Singapore for more than 183 days in a year) can
claim for tax relief.
Please check if you have met the qualifying conditions of the reliefs before making a claim for them. To find out more,
click on the different reliefs below. A personal income tax relief cap of $80,000 applies to the total amount of all tax
reliefs claimed for each Year of Assessment (YA).
